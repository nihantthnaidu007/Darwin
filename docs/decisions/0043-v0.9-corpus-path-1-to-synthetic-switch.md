# 0043 v0.9 corpus path: switch from real open-source sourcing (Path-1) to the synthetic reference-first path

Decision: The v0.9 corpus is built on the synthetic, reference-first path: the author writes a clean `reference.py`, and the `bloat` tool degrades it into the messy `original.py`. This SUPERSEDES the Path-1 contract committed at P9.0, which specified sampling real, permissively-licensed open-source functions and authoring a clean reference for each. The synthetic methodology contract (docs/darwin-v0.9-corpus-methodology-synthetic.md) replaces the Path-1 contract (docs/darwin-v0.9-corpus-methodology.md) and was committed before any new case was built. The Path-1 construction tool (src/darwin/build_real_case.py) and its license gate, built and tested in P9.1 and P9.2a, are retained in the tree as completed work, superseded for this milestone.

Context: Path-1 set out to ground the one-shot finding on real-world code so the corpus would not be open to the charge of being authored to flatter the result. Building the pilot exposed a structural tension in that path before any real case was authored: clean, well-licensed library functions have a near-zero optimization span (they are clean precisely because they are maintained), while messy functions with a real span tend to carry unusable provenance (tutorial or recipe code with murky licensing). Real, pure, permissively-licensed, AND meaningfully-improvable standalone functions are a narrow and slow-to-source population. Outcome-blind real-world sourcing therefore could not produce a corpus of measurable cases without selection bias: to get a measurable span the author would have to hand-pick messy code, which reintroduces exactly the outcome-selection the outcome-blind rule was meant to forbid. The pilot did its job: it surfaced the methodology cost before the human sourcing effort was spent.

The synthetic path resolves the tension by construction. A clean reference is authored to a readability rubric that is independent of what a model can reach in one generation, and the `bloat` tool (not the author) injects the mess that defines the original. The category composition is fixed before building, references are committed before their runs (temporal freeze), and the full distribution is reported, so the integrity guarantees survive the switch even though the source code is now synthetic.

Alternatives rejected:
- Stay on Path-1 and source real functions: rejected. The clean-but-no-span versus messy-but-no-provenance tension has no outcome-blind resolution at single-function scope; any real corpus large enough to measure would require hand-selecting for span, which is the selection bias the contract forbids.
- Hand-author the originals directly (skip the `bloat` tool): rejected. A hand-written original chosen by the author is an original chosen to be reversible, putting the injected mess under the author's outcome control. Tool-generated degradation keeps the mess out of the author's hands.
- Delete build_real_case.py and the license gate as dead code: rejected. They are completed, tested work that correctly implements the Path-1 construction; removing them would erase the record that the path was built and tried. They are retained-but-superseded, not orphaned, and this record is the documented reason they stay.

Consequences: The v0.9 corpus is synthetic: every case is a hand-authored reference degraded by a tool, not real-world code. This is the headline limitation, stated on every results surface (reports/RESULTS.md, the dataset, and CONTEXT). The real-world, open-source, and production claims are explicitly out of scope for v0.9 (decision 0045). build_real_case.py and its license gate remain in src/darwin/ as retained-but-superseded Path-1 tooling, tested and unused by the v0.9 corpus; if a future milestone returns to real-world sourcing, they are the starting point. No optimization, scoring, equivalence, or default-lockdown behavior changed: this is a corpus-construction-path decision. The seventh degradation category (decision 0044) and the corpus-expansion finding (decision 0045) build on this synthetic path.
